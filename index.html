Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Do you handle both trade and technical advisory?

Yes. That's exactly our strength. We connect companies with suppliers and markets, while applying technical expertise to projects at the same time. The two reinforce each other: we understand both the market and the product.

What exactly is Trade & Business Matchmaking?

We connect foreign companies with trusted Dutch partners — and vice versa — with particular expertise in electrical, hydraulic, mechanical, and safety engineering. This service builds on the hands-on experience from Matozzi Tecnica, where we manage international sourcing and certification ourselves on a daily basis.

Which sectors are you active in?

Our roots lie in offshore, marine, energy and industry — sectors where engineering, safety and reliability are central. Our trade activities are more broadly applicable.

Do you also work with international parties?

Absolutely. We support Dutch companies in international growth and help foreign companies gain access to the Dutch and European market.

How do we start working together?

Get in touch and tell us what you're looking for. We'll discuss your request, no obligation, and propose a tailor-made approach.
